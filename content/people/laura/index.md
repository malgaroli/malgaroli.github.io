Laura Bragdon is a Clinical Assistant Professor of Psychiatry at NYU Grossman School of Medicine. She received her Ph.D. in clinical psychology from Binghamton University (SUNY) and completed her pre-doctoral internship at the VA Boston Healthcare System. Dr. Bragdon completed a research postdoctoral fellowship at NYU Grossman School of Medicine focused on the neural and behavioral mechanisms underlying OCD and related disorders. Her clinical and research interests include clarifying mechanisms of empirically-supported treatments and expanding access to effective therapies for anxiety disorders, OCD, and related conditions.
