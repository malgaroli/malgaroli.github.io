We develop machine learning methods that move beyond diagnostic categories to characterize how symptoms are organized and how they change over time. This includes [VISTA-SSM](https://github.com/benjaminbrindle/vista_ssm), our clustering method for noisy, irregular, and incomplete longitudinal data, and trajectory modeling that identifies heterogeneous courses of adjustment to stress, loss, and treatment and the predictors that distinguish them. Complementing these person-centered approaches, we use network analysis to map empirical relationships among symptoms and, most recently, to characterize the temporal dynamics of patient emotions turn by turn in very large psychotherapy samples.
